# Blog Detail Mobile Figma Inspection Notes

## Whole-frame hierarchy

The selected Figma frame is a very tall, narrow mobile article page. It preserves the desktop content sequence in a compact single-column editorial layout: mobile header, article category/title/author metadata, lead image, full article flow, Key Takeaways, Related Solutions, Leave a Comment, related reading, contained conversion panel, and footer. The desktop sticky contents rail does not appear in the mobile frame. The mobile layout relies on generous vertical rhythm, small lime eyebrow labels, white Chivo display headings, dark low-radius cards, and single-column reading modules.

## Verified mid-page modules

At readable Figma scale, article prose is compact but comfortably separated into single-column `Phase` sections. The paired MVP evidence tiles remain two-up on mobile. Key Takeaways is a dark contained card with three individually separated low-contrast rows and lime check-circle markers. Related Solutions follows immediately with three shallow dark link rows and small right-pointing arrows. The Leave a Comment panel uses a dark bordered card that spans the reading column; this current structure should remain, with mobile spacing calibrated to the reference.

Mobile article section headings are approximately 18–20px relative to the 320px design width, while body copy is about 10px with leading near 1.5. The mobile data pair remains wide and shallow with lime figures. Key Takeaways and Related Solutions are visually left-aligned to the article content rather than full-bleed; their rows have notably compact vertical spacing.
