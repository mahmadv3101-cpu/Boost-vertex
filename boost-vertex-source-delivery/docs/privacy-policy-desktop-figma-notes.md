# Privacy Policy Desktop Figma Inspection Notes

## Artboard Overview

- Desktop reference frame is approximately **900 × 2780 px** within the Figma canvas and uses the existing Boost Vertex dark obsidian/lime system.
- The page begins with the shared desktop navigation, followed by a wide electric-lime policy hero with a centered black **Privacy Policy** title and a short explanatory subtitle.
- The content body is an asymmetric layout: a narrow left table-of-contents rail, a wide central policy article, and a slim right floating Fast Track action rail.
- The legal article contains ten numbered sections, each using a bold white heading, a restrained lime numerical accent, and compact muted-gray paragraphs with list indenting.
- The lower page includes a dark bordered conversion card titled **Initiate Strategic Engagement** before the shared footer. The desktop header, action rail, and footer follow the approved Boost Vertex language rather than introducing a separate privacy-page style.

## Focused Top Section

- At the Figma desktop reference width, a full-width black header has a lime **Boost Vertex** wordmark on the left and the standard center links: Services, Case Studies, About, Blog, and Contact. It also includes the phone number and a lime conversion button at the right.
- The lime hero is inset horizontally by approximately 40 px from the page boundary and uses about 126 px height. Its centered title is black, bold Chivo, approximately 32 px. The centered copy reads: **We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.** followed by **Last Updated: October 24, 2024**.
- The article begins after a generous 28–32 px top gap. It sits in a 3-column shell: 176 px dark table-of-contents card at left; an approximately 560 px content column in the middle; a fixed Fast Track action rail at the right side of the viewport.
- The table-of-contents card title is uppercase **TABLE OF CONTENTS** and visibly lists: Introduction, Information We Collect, How We Use Information, Cookies & Tracking, Data Sharing, Data Security, Data Retention, Your Rights, Third-Party Services, Policy Updates, and Contact Us.
- The visible article text is compact Inter body at approximately 12 px / 18 px, with bold 24 px Chivo headings. The first sections read **1. Introduction**, **2. Information We Collect**, **3. How We Use Information**, then **4. Cookies & Tracking**.

## Focused Middle Section

- **Information We Collect** is structured with four bold inline labels: Identity Data, Contact Data, Technical Data, and Usage Data.
- **How We Use Information** uses a short intro followed by four indented plain-text list items for marketing consultancy delivery, relationship management, administration/protection, and analytics.
- **Cookies & Tracking**, **Data Sharing**, **Data Security**, and **Data Retention** preserve the same continuous legal-article rhythm; no divider lines separate sections. Headings use roughly 25–30 px vertical top spacing, while body paragraphs remain tightly stacked below.
- The table-of-contents card remains aligned with the early article content rather than continuing as a viewport-sticky element through the whole document.

## Focused Lower Section

- **Data Retention** is followed by **Your Rights**, whose body introduces a compact indented list: request access, correction, erasure, object to processing, request restriction, and request transfer.
- **Third-Party Services** follows with a single compact paragraph. **Policy Updates** follows at the bottom of the legal article.
- The content rail remains intentionally narrow and left-anchored inside the central portion of the desktop page; the large empty region to the right is part of the Figma composition and should not be filled with an additional card or visual.

## End-of-Page Treatment

- **10. Policy Updates** closes the article with one short paragraph. A faint horizontal rule precedes the conversion card.
- The conversion card has a dark graphite surface, restrained 1 px border, 12 px corner radius, and the title **Initiate Strategic Engagement**. Its copy references privacy, compliance protocols, and exercising rights under applicable regulations. It includes the lime email link **privacy@boostvertex.com** and a lime rounded **Contact Privacy Team** button aligned at the right.
- The shared footer starts immediately below a full-width divider. It uses the existing Boost Vertex wordmark at left and grouped navigation columns at right, consistent with the other approved desktop pages.

## Mobile Figma Overview

- The mobile reference is a very tall single-column legal page, approximately 390 px wide, with the compact shared mobile header, a lime policy banner, then the full article in continuous vertical flow.
- The desktop table-of-contents card is absent in the mobile frame. The content begins directly with **1. Introduction** below the compact banner and proceeds through the ten sections in order.
- The reference includes the compact privacy engagement panel shortly after **10. Policy Updates**, then a condensed mobile footer. A fixed four-action Call/Chat/Book/Inquiry rail appears at the bottom of the viewport and must clear scrolling content.
- Focused inspection is required for the exact banner scale, content width, section rhythm, engagement-card stacking, and footer compression.

## Focused Mobile Top Section

- The mobile frame retains a short black header with lime **Boost Vertex** branding and a compact right-side menu treatment.
- The lime banner is inset by roughly 14–18 px, short and left-aligned rather than centered. It uses a bold black **Privacy Policy** heading, two-line explanatory copy, and a small **Last Updated: October 24, 2024** line.
- Contrary to the overview-scale read, the mobile frame does include a full-width dark **TABLE OF CONTENTS** card directly after the banner. It lists all eleven items in a single compact vertical column rather than the desktop side rail.
- After a deliberate dark gap, the article becomes a single narrow column with a larger white **1. Introduction** heading. The body is compact but readable, and **2. Information We Collect** wraps naturally across two lines at mobile width. The desktop floating Fast Track rail does not appear beside this mobile article; the established site-wide bottom mobile rail remains the appropriate action treatment.

## Focused Mobile Article Flow

- The mobile legal article uses approximately 18–20 px Chivo section headings with a warm off-white color. Headings wrap only when needed, with **Information We Collect** and **How We Use Information** breaking at natural word boundaries.
- Paragraph copy is substantially smaller than the headings and uses a muted gray-green tone with close but readable line height. Each numbered section has around 24–30 px of vertical breathing room before the next section.
- Long paragraphs and the rights list remain fully single-column without desktop side spacing. The document is intentionally very tall; the Figma does not reduce or omit policy content on mobile.

## Focused Mobile End Treatment

- **10. Policy Updates** is followed by a full-width dark engagement card with approximately 12 px internal radius. The title wraps to two lines as **Initiate Strategic Engagement**; the email appears beneath the muted explanatory copy. The lime **Contact Privacy Team** button is positioned to the card's right side rather than becoming full width.
- The mobile footer is compressed into three adjacent columns—Services, Company, and Connect—using very small uppercase labels and short stacked links. It starts after a thin horizontal divider and sits above the fixed mobile Fast Track tray.
