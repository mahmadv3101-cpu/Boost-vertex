# Thank You Desktop Figma Inspection Notes

## Artboard Overview

- The desktop reference is a compact, approximately 900 px wide single-page confirmation experience using the existing Boost Vertex obsidian, pale text, and electric-lime design system.
- It uses the shared compact desktop header, then a centered confirmation hero with a small outlined lime check circle, large white **Thank You—We’ve Got Your Request.** heading, and a short reassurance paragraph.
- A pair of tight centered action buttons follows: lime **Back to Home** and dark outlined **Explore Our Services**.
- The page includes a horizontal **While You Wait** utility section with three dark cards: Case Studies, Services, and Insights.
- A lime action band follows: **Ready to scale immediately?** with short supporting copy and a dark phone CTA.
- The compact shared footer finishes the page, with a Boost Vertex brand/newsletter area and three nav groups. The mobile version is visible beside the desktop frame but is not in scope yet.

Focused inspection is needed next to record exact hero spacing, card copy, button labels, action-band proportions, and footer detail.

## Focused Hero and Utility Section

The desktop header uses the approved 80 px black Boost Vertex navigation. Below it, the confirmation hero has approximately 75 px of top clearance before a 54 px outlined circle with a lime checkmark. The two-line white Chivo headline reads **Thank You—We’ve Got Your Request.** Its display scale is approximately 45–50 px, and the paired em dash remains on the first line. The supporting copy reads: **Our team is reviewing your details and will be in touch shortly to discuss how we can elevate your performance and drive scalable results.**

The actions are 32 px high with compact horizontal padding. The primary lime action reads **Back to Home →**. The outlined secondary action reads **Explore Our Services →**. A large intentional dark gap separates the action pair from the **While You Wait** label, which precedes three equal dark cards.

The three cards carry the labels **Case Studies**, **Services**, and **Insights**. Their descriptions are respectively: **See how we’ve driven growth for industry leaders.**; **Explore our comprehensive suite of performance marketing solutions.**; and **Read the latest strategies and industry trends from our experts.** Each card uses a small pale line icon, bold white Chivo label, compact muted copy, 1 px charcoal border, and shallow 5 px corner radius.

## Focused Action Band and Footer

The utility cards are followed by roughly 78 px of vertical space, then a 260 × 86 px electric-lime rounded action band centered within the content width. It reads **Ready to scale immediately?** with the supporting copy **Don’t wait for our email. Call us directly to expedite your consultation.** A compact dark button at the right reads **Call +1 (234) 567-890**.

The footer begins after another approximately 64 px gap and a faint top divider. Unlike the generic shared footer, the Thank You footer starts with a lime Boost Vertex wordmark, a compact brand statement, and a small **Subscribe to our insights** email field with lime **Join** action. Services, Company, and Connect form three compact adjacent groups. The legal bar closes the page with the copyright notice at left and Privacy Policy/Terms of Service at right.

## Mobile Figma Overview

The mobile reference is a short, dense single-column confirmation page with a compact header, centered confirmation hero, stacked actions, a vertically ordered resource list, small lime immediate-call card, compact newsletter footer, and the shared four-action bottom Fast Track rail. The desktop hero-side Fast Track rail is absent at mobile width.

The resource card order changes on mobile: **Insights** is first, followed by **Case Studies**, then **Services**. The desktop three-card row becomes three shallow full-width cards. The mobile footer retains the Boost Vertex wordmark, newsletter input, mini navigation groups, and legal information above the fixed bottom rail.

## Focused Mobile Hero

The mobile header is approximately 52 px high, with the lime wordmark at left and a three-line lime hamburger at right. The hero has a generous dark field under the header. A glowing filled dark-lime 52 px check disc appears above a two-line white Chivo heading: **Thank You — We’ve Got Your Request.**

The mobile supporting copy is different from desktop: **Our strategic consultants are reviewing your details. We’ll be in touch within 24 hours to discuss how we can elevate your performance.** The actions are full-width and vertically stacked: lime **Back to Home →**, then dark outlined **Explore Our Services →**. A thin divider leads into the **While You Wait** title.

The first mobile resource card is **Latest Insights**, with a lime outlined document icon and the copy **Read our latest strategies for scaling performance.** Cards are shallow, full-width, and separated by roughly 8 px.

## Focused Mobile Lower Content

The full resource sequence is **Latest Insights** — *Read our latest strategies for scaling performance.*; **Case Studies** — *See how we’ve driven growth for industry leaders.*; and **Services** — *Explore our comprehensive suite of performance marketing solutions.* The three cards are approximately 52 px tall with 9 px horizontal icon/text spacing.

The desktop immediate-call band becomes a dark olive/lime radial card reading **Ready to scale?** with **Follow us on LinkedIn for daily performance marketing tips.** Its action is a simple lime text link: **Connect with us ↗**. The small card is placed after a substantial dark gap, before the compact footer. The shared fixed Call/Chat/Book/Inquiry action rail stays visible at the mobile viewport bottom.
