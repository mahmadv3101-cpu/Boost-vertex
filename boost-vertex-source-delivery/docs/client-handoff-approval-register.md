3. **Provide official contact details** and the final Fast Track destinations for phone, WhatsApp, chat, calendar, and enquiry actions.
4. **Provide official logo files and brand guidelines**, including light/dark variants and favicon guidance. The currently supplied logo image is integrated, but the formal brand package remains useful for final production consistency.
5. **Provide client logos, project images, and permissions** for MovePro Pakistan, Dr. Waqas Ahmad, and Whizpool.
6. **Provide legal documents and form consent language** for Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookies, and data collection.
7. **Provide the deployed backend API URL and endpoint documentation** so forms, newsletter actions, comments, and dynamic content can be connected and tested.
